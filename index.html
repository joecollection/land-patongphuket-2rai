Patong is Phuket's highest-traffic zone. 8.8M annual arrivals, 79.5%+ hotel occupancy and growing digital-nomad / long-stay segment provide structural demand floor.

Title & Ownership Risk
Nor Sor 3 Kor freehold is the highest-quality Thai title. Recommend standard due diligence: title search, encumbrance check, land boundary survey.

Zoning / Height Legislation
60m height uplift is proposed, not yet enacted. Current Commercial Zone already permits viable development. New zoning is upside optionality, not base case dependency.

Construction Cost & Timeline
Material cost inflation and contractor availability in Phuket remain watch items. Fixed-price EPC contract with reputable contractor strongly recommended. Buffer 15% contingency.
Medium Risk
Currency & FX Risk
USD/THB fluctuations affect foreign investor capital deployment. Thai Baht has shown relative stability. Revenue is THB-denominated; natural hedge for local operators.

Market Competition
41 hotel projects in Phuket pipeline 2026–2030 are primarily in Bang Tao and Kamala. Patong CBD new supply is extremely limited — competitive moat is structural.
